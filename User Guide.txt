Toss 1.0.8 — User Guide

One app, one installation
The menu bar icon, connection panel, screenshot service, and runtime are all included in Toss.app. No separate menu bar component is needed. The icon appears whenever Toss is running; closing Settings keeps it running.

Install
1. Open the disk image and double-click Toss Installer. In its window, drag Toss into Applications; setup opens automatically.
2. This test build is not notarized by Apple. If macOS blocks it, review the notice in System Settings → Privacy & Security and use Open Anyway if you trust this build.
3. If Toss asks, click Install and Open. It copies the complete app to Applications and removes the download quarantine flag from this app only. macOS may ask for your Mac password. If administrator access is declined, Toss can use your personal Applications folder.
4. The menu bar feature is ready as soon as Toss opens. No extra install is required.

Requirements
• Apple Silicon Mac (M1 or later), macOS 14 or later.
• iPhone or iPad running iOS or iPadOS 17 or later.
• A USB data cable for the first pairing.
• Both devices on the same local network. Your Mac may use Wi-Fi or Ethernet; your iPhone or iPad may use Wi-Fi or a hotspot. Guest and campus networks may block device discovery.
• ChatGPT / Codex and/or Claude desktop app.

First setup
Until at least one iPhone or iPad is paired, opening Toss from the Dock or clicking its menu-bar icon reopens the setup guide, even if it was closed before.
Connect your iPhone or iPad via USB and unlock it. Toss opens its setup guide; follow the steps. Tap Trust and enter the passcode if your iPhone or iPad asks. Keep USB connected until pairing is complete. The first developer image download needs internet access.

Developer Mode is also required. Its switch stays hidden on the device until Toss shows it, which happens once the device is plugged in and setup has started, so plug in first. Each device is set up on its own. If the guide doesn't open by itself, open Toss Settings → Setup guide. Then on your iPhone or iPad, open Settings → Privacy & Security (设置 → 隐私与安全性), scroll to the very bottom and tap Developer Mode (开发者模式). Turn it on and tap Restart. After the restart, unlock your iPhone or iPad, tap Turn On and enter the passcode.

Don't see Developer Mode? Swipe Settings away from the app switcher and open it again. Settings doesn't show the new row while it stays open in the background.

Allow Accessibility access for Toss in System Settings → Privacy & Security → Accessibility. Confirm with Touch ID or your password. Input Monitoring is not required. Allow local network access if macOS asks. Shortcuts enable automatically when pairing and permissions are ready.

Capture into the current chat
• Press left and right Option together for ChatGPT / Codex.
• Press left and right Shift together for Claude.
• With both devices paired, press the shortcut once for iPad or twice quickly for iPhone. If one device is locked or has been offline for six seconds, either shortcut uses the other device if it's connected and hasn't reported that it's locked.
• Release the keys after pressing. Keep your iPhone or iPad unlocked.
Toss brings the target app forward and adds one new screenshot to its current chat. It preserves the existing draft and attachments. Review the attachment and send the message yourself. It never sends automatically or retries a paste that may already have happened.

Trigger Toss from iPhone
1. Pair the iPhone in Toss on the Mac. Toss fills in the Apple Shortcuts setup form. Click Add Shortcut and allow access if asked. This check runs on the Mac. If Shortcuts sync is on, the configured shortcut can then appear on iPhone.
2. On iPhone, open Settings → Accessibility → Touch → Back Tap. Choose Toss iPhone under Double Tap or Triple Tap. Turn off Show Banner on the Back Tap screen to keep its notice out of screenshots. On an iPhone with an Action button, you can also select Toss iPhone in Settings → Action Button → Shortcut.
3. If Toss iPhone is missing on your phone, or to update an older Toss Tap, open Toss Settings → More → iPhone Shortcut → Pair on iPhone on your Mac. Click Copy pairing configuration to use Universal Clipboard. Without Universal Clipboard, click AirDrop pairing text, send it to your own iPhone, open Toss Pairing.txt in Files and copy the entire line.
4. Click Send via AirDrop in the same Mac window to send the Toss Tap shortcut to the iPhone. Paste the configuration when Shortcuts asks during setup, then tap Add Shortcut. Choose Replace if Toss Tap is already installed. You can now delete the pairing text file if you received one.
5. Use the iPhone Camera to scan the verification code in the Mac window. Tap the prompt to run Toss Tap and choose Always Allow if Shortcuts asks to connect to your Mac. The window closes when the Mac verifies the phone. On iPhone, choose the new Toss Tap in Settings → Accessibility → Touch → Back Tap → Double Tap. Open the page you want to capture and double-tap the back of the phone.

Keep the iPhone unlocked, connect both devices to the same local network, and leave Toss shortcuts enabled and the chosen chat app open on the Mac. The shortcut captures the paired iPhone and adds the screenshot to the current draft. Change the destination or turn off the phone trigger in Toss Settings → More → iPhone Shortcut.

Menu bar status
Click the Toss icon to see iPad connection status and screenshot progress. The icon is the ⌥ symbol with a solid up arrow. While a screenshot is uploading, only the arrow moves up and down, blue when sending to Codex and orange when sending to Claude; the ⌥ stays still. When processing ends, the arrow stops and returns to its normal colour.

The Last screenshot section shows its destination, received file size, total time from shortcut to result, and the USB or Wi-Fi route actually used. Success means the attachment was found in the draft. Other outcomes say Not successful, with a short explanation for an unconfirmed paste, manual paste, cancellation, or no paste request. An unconfirmed paste may still be in the draft, so check before trying again. If the screenshot was only copied, the destination is Clipboard. Missing measurements are omitted. The last result stays available while Toss is running, including after a connection change.

Check Connection checks the existing pairing without taking or pasting a screenshot. Connection errors appear inside the panel. Use Settings for pairing and permissions, or Diagnostics for technical details. Right-click the icon to access the full menu, including Quit Toss.

Close the status panel with its × button, Escape, another click on the icon, or a click outside. It also closes when you switch apps. If a crowded menu bar or the camera housing hides the icon, Connection Status in Toss's application menu opens the panel near the top-right corner instead of anchoring it to an invisible icon.

Launch at Login
Toss remembers whether shortcuts are enabled and offers Launch at Login. Disabling shortcuts stops automatic resumption; quitting preserves your setting. Turning off Launch at Login does not turn off shortcuts in the current session. No screenshot is taken on launch, reconnect, or wake.

If a capture fails
Open Diagnostics, or choose Copy Diagnostics when you want to share the report. It contains status, permission checks, timings, and sanitized error codes, not chat text, screenshots, or device keys. Capture failures do not show an alert; a failed keypress plays one soft, low two-note sound (a cancel caused by your own typing or clicking stays silent). If paste was requested but not confirmed, inspect the draft before trying again.

Limitations
The target chat, composer, and draft must be verifiable. Unsupported layouts, ambiguous windows, selected text, an open modal, or changes during capture may stop the operation. Claude project pages are not supported. Wait for a Codex response to finish before capturing. Keep the iPad unlocked; lock-screen detection cannot reliably identify every locked or protected screen.

The menu panel and English UI were checked on this Mac. This update does not establish a fresh end-to-end iPad capture test; wireless capture must be verified with a reachable iPad. Network changes, long idle periods, and other device models may need additional testing.

Privacy and files
Toss requests one screenshot per shortcut; it does not continuously record the iPad screen. It uses authenticated pairing and local communication. With iPhone Shortcut enabled, the Mac accepts a local trigger authorized by the setup code. The screenshot uses the existing paired connection. Pairing files are stored in ~/Library/Application Support/Toss/DeviceState and should not be shared. Screenshot caches are in ~/Library/Caches/Toss. Do not share these folders in diagnostics.

Uninstall
Open Toss → Settings → Uninstall Toss… and confirm Uninstall. Toss stops its capture and pairing processes, attempts to unregister its login item, removes its local pairing files, settings and screenshot cache, then moves itself to the Trash and quits. If it cannot move itself, Finder selects the app and asks you to drag Toss to the Trash. The Accessibility permission is kept so a reinstall works at once; you can remove it in System Settings → Privacy & Security → Accessibility. Trust and Developer Mode settings on your iPad are unchanged.
